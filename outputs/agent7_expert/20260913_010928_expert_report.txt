SECTION 8 — DIFFERENTIAL DIAGNOSES
=================================================================
1. **Atypical (dysplastic) nevus** – a mole that looks a little irregular in shape or color. It can look like a normal mole but may have a higher chance of becoming cancerous, so it needs a closer look.  
2. **Seborrheic keratosis (benign keratosis)** – a harmless, waxy‑looking growth that can be brown or black. It can be mistaken for a mole because of its color.  
3. **Melanoma** – a serious skin cancer that can look similar to a regular mole when it is early. It is important to rule this out because it can spread quickly.  
4. **Vascular lesion** – a birthmark or spot made of blood vessels that may appear red, purple, or dark. It can sometimes be confused with a pigmented mole.  
5. **Inflammatory lesion** – a spot caused by irritation or infection (e.g., a wart or eczema patch). It may change color and needs to be checked if it looks like a mole.
