 Mitchell. January 2021. Introduction Demographics Causes Clinical features Dermoscopic features Complications Diagnosis Differential diagnoses Treatment Outcome What is a pyogenic granuloma? Pyogenic granuloma is an acquired benign proliferation of capillary blood vessels of the skin and oral cavity. The name is a misnomer as it is a form of lobular capillary haemangioma , not due to infection. Pyogenic granuloma has many synonyms including granuloma gravidarum or pregnancy tumour when occurring in pregnancy .
